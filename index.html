Flippin' Fast Privacy Policy

Pravaah Tech Solutions · Last updated 2 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Flippin' Fast (package com.pravaahtechsolutions.flippinfast) handles, why, and your choices. Flippin' Fast is made by Pravaah Tech Solutions ("we", "us").

Summary

  • You don't create an account, and we don't ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device.
  • Coin purchases are handled by Google Play; we never see your payment details.
  • The game engine (Unity) may send crash and device diagnostics to help fix problems.
  • The game currently shows no ads.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your device: coins, unlocked cars and drivers, upgrades, missions completed and settings. This file stays on your device and is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

In-app purchases

You can buy coin packs with real money. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy. We don't receive your card or payment details. The game receives a purchase confirmation and its order ID from Google Play, and keeps the order ID on your device so the same purchase is never credited twice.

Diagnostics from the game engine

Flippin' Fast is built with the Unity engine. Unity Technologies may automatically collect technical information when the game runs or crashes: device model, operating system version, hardware details, app version, crash reports and performance data. Unity uses it to provide and improve its engine services. See Unity's privacy policy for game players.

Advertising

The game shows no ads at the moment. If we add ads in a future update, we will update this policy first to explain what the ad networks collect and your choices.

Sharing

We don't sell your information. Apart from Google Play (purchases) and Unity (diagnostics) as described above, we don't share information with anyone.

Children

Flippin' Fast is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Delete your saved progress by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game.
  • Manage purchases and refunds in your Google Play account.
  • For questions about Unity's diagnostics, see Unity's privacy policy above.

Security

Your progress stays on your device, and payments go through Google Play's secure billing. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we keep the information we handle to the minimum the game needs.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add features or ads. The date at the top shows the latest version, and significant changes will be announced in the game's release notes.
